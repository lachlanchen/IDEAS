# OrganoidVision: Engineering Retinal Organoids as Advanced Imaging Sensors

Author: Lachlan Chen, AgInTiFlow
Affiliation: AgInTi Lab, LazyingArt LLC

## Concept

OrganoidVision reframes the former OrganoidSensor idea as a living vision platform: a retinal organoid is not only a biological sample, but an adaptive imaging front end that can receive optical stimuli, compress visual dynamics into sparse electrophysiological events, and hand those events to neuromorphic decoders.

The imported publication describes a full stack for this direction: cone-enriched retinal organoid morphogenesis, light conditioning, 3D liquid-metal microelectrode arrays, perfused microfluidics, optogenetic shunts, event-based decoding, non-destructive quality control, and ethics-by-design deployment boundaries.

## Product Direction

- Build a bio-hybrid camera architecture where retinal organoids perform low-power visual preprocessing before silicon decoding.
- Treat the electrophysiology stream as event-based visual data instead of dense frames.
- Use microfluidics, impedance diagnostics, metabolic telemetry, FFOCT, hyperspectral imaging, and adaptive calibration to keep the organoid stable over multi-week operation.
- Package the research into a reproducible OrganoidVision publication and future simulation/control code.
